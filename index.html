Nine months after setting foot in his first acting class, Isa got a call to audition for an original play about the Palestinian-Israeli conflict. Despite never having auditioned before, he prepared a monologue, a headshot and a blank acting resume. He got the job as both an actor and a member of the writing team.

The company was the San Francisco Mime Troupe, where “mime” means storytelling with words and songs. The play opened to a record 4,000 people in Dolores Park, toured the U.S. and abroad for two years, and won the Obie Award for its New York run.

Isa then moved to Los Angeles to study with the legendary acting teacher and director Milton Katselas, and stayed for well over a decade. Katselas mentored him as both a director and an actor, and asked him to teach acting at his school.

In recent years Isa has changed his focus from the stage to the screen, writing and directing, while teaching acting in his spare time.

About the talks

Isa is a public speaker with over three decades of experience inspiring audiences across the globe. He has given live and virtual seminars in 18 countries.

Known for turning seminars into immersive experiences that blend storytelling, multimedia and insight, his talks on art, imagination and aesthetics help audiences discover their own creativity and break through limitations.

With his wife, Allison Totah, he founded the Totah Foundation for the Arts, a non-profit dedicated to building a better world through the power of art.

    About

    Isa Totah

    Isa Totah is a multi-disciplinary artist, filmmaker, actor, speaker, writer, and visual storyteller whose work spans film, theater, painting, and photography.

    At the center of it is one idea: storytelling that carries hope. Isa is a writer-director whose films and screenplays explore conflict, relationships, and the search for meaning, always with an underlying optimism about people.

    After more than thirty years working across stage, screen, and visual art, his focus is making films. He also speaks and consults on artistic careers and on the role of aesthetics and creativity in a life well lived.

    Born in Jerusalem and raised in San Francisco’s Sunset District, Isa now works in Los Angeles. His work, whether on screen or in conversation, is rooted in the conviction that art can affirm the human spirit and make the world a little better.
